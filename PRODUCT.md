# Linebreak

## Platform
web

## Stack
React, TypeScript, Vite, Cloudflare Workers, SQLite Durable Objects, WebSocket Hibernation, Vitest.

## Users and purpose
Two people playing a casual strategy game together, on one screen or on separate devices. Race from the same side to the top edge, moving or placing a fixed two-edge wall each turn.

## Rules and constraints
A begins at (8,2), B at (8,6), both target row 0, A starts every round. Unlimited walls. Crossings and touching permitted; overlapping edges and eliminating either player's last route prohibited. Jump and sidestep movement. Guest identity only. No bots, rankings, chat, paid services, or purchased domain. Reconnection and real authoritative multiplayer required.

## Brand commitments
Linebreak. Restrained digital board-game aesthetic, neutral warm background, charcoal walls, teal A and rust B. Board first, concise copy, precise touch and keyboard input. No dashboard scaffolding.

## Lifecycle
Pause on disconnect; connected opponent may claim after 90 seconds. Both players request an online rematch; seats stay fixed. Offline local play after initial caching.
